Hi, I'm Renan, a software engineer. I've been building software since 2017.
Before that, I spent a few years in industrial automation programming PLCs and
HMIs, which is where I learned that code can move real things.

I've always loved building things. Engineering is often seen as an exact
discipline, but to me building is a creative process, and it only works when you
understand the people you're building for. That's exactly what drew me to
programming: it lets one idea reach a lot of people.

These days I work mostly on the web, but I have a soft spot for projects that
bring software and hardware together, where code leaves the screen and interacts
with the physical world. The biggest one (which has turned into a hobby of its
own) is a Portuguese-language voice assistant, a sort of homemade alternative to
Alexa or Google Home.

## A note on language
